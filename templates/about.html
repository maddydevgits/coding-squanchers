Advanced Technology

Our platform leverages state-of-the-art sensors and AI to provide comprehensive sleep analysis

ESP32 Microcontroller

High-performance microcontroller with WiFi connectivity for real-time data transmission and processing.

  • Dual-core processor
  • WiFi & Bluetooth
  • Low power consumption

MAX30102 Sensor

Advanced optical sensor for continuous heart rate and blood oxygen saturation monitoring during sleep.

  • Heart rate monitoring
  • SpO2 measurement
  • Non-invasive detection

BMP280 Sensor

Precision environmental sensor measuring temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure for optimal sleep conditions.

  • Temperature sensing
  • Humidity monitoring
  • Pressure measurement

DHT11 Sensor

Additional temperature and humidity sensor providing redundant measurements for enhanced accuracy and reliability.

  • Dual sensor validation
  • Cost-effective solution
  • High accuracy

Flame Detection

Safety-first approach with advanced flame detection sensors to ensure a secure sleep environment.

  • Fire safety
  • Instant alerts
  • Emergency notifications
